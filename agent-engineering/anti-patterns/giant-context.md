# Giant Context

## Signal

History and raw tool results accumulate indefinitely in the prompt.

## Why it fails

It increases cost, latency, nondeterminism, and incident surface while obscuring accountability.

## Reject or correct

Externalize artifacts, retrieve/filter decision slices, extract canonical state, and compact with provenance.

## Required guardrail

Never compact away evidence needed for an immediate high-impact decision.

## Review evidence

Ask for the state model, policy boundary, budget, trace, and a test that demonstrates the guardrail.
